Who We Are

PDF Toolkit Demo is a free, easy-to-use online PDF tool platform. Our mission is simple: give everyone access to powerful PDF tools without requiring any software installation, account creation, or payment.

What We Offer

We offer 17 free PDF tools including Merge PDF, Split PDF, Compress PDF, Rotate PDF, Protect PDF, Unlock PDF, Watermark PDF, Image to PDF, PDF to Image, Add Page Numbers, Extract Text (OCR), Repair PDF, Compare PDF, and more. All tools work directly in your browser — no plugins or apps required.

Our Commitment to Privacy

We take your privacy seriously. Files you upload are processed immediately and never stored on our servers. As soon as your file is ready to download, it is deleted from our system. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Our Goal

We believe PDF tools should be accessible to everyone — students, professionals, freelancers, and businesses alike. That's why every tool on PDF Toolkit Demo is completely free, with no hidden fees, no watermarks added to your files, and no daily usage limits.
