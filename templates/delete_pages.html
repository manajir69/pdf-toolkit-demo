Frequently Asked Questions

How do I specify which pages to delete?
Enter the page numbers separated by commas, and use a hyphen for a continuous run. For example 2, 5, 9-12 removes page two, page five, and pages nine through twelve. Everything you do not list is kept.
Do the page numbers shift as pages are deleted?
No, and this is the detail that trips people up elsewhere. All your numbers refer to the original document as it stands right now. Deleting page three does not renumber page four to three mid-operation — the whole selection is evaluated against the original before anything is removed.
What is the difference between deleting and extracting pages?
Deleting keeps everything except the pages you name. Extract Pages does the opposite: it keeps only the pages you name and discards the rest. Choose whichever means typing fewer numbers — if you want 3 pages out of 200, extract; if you want to lose 3 out of 200, delete.
Can I automatically delete all the blank pages?
Not from this page — here you specify page numbers by hand. For automatic detection use Remove Blank Pages, which scans the document, finds every empty page itself, and removes them all in one pass.
Will deleting pages affect the quality of the remaining ones?
No. The pages you keep are copied across untouched with their original resolution, embedded fonts, and images fully intact. Only the page objects you removed are dropped, so file size goes down while quality stays exactly the same.
Can I delete pages from a password-protected PDF?
Yes. If your file is encrypted, a password prompt appears automatically after upload. Enter it once and the operation proceeds normally. To remove the password permanently instead, run the file through our Unlock PDF tool first.

Almost every real document contains pages nobody needs. A cover sheet that served its purpose, a blank separator between sections, a duplicate that got scanned twice, an internal note that should not go to the client. This tool removes them: enter the page numbers, download the trimmed file, done. It is free, needs no software, and leaves the remaining pages completely untouched.

Why Remove Pages?

Confidentiality is the most important reason. Internal comments, pricing breakdowns, and draft notes frequently sit inside documents that later go to an outside party. Removing those pages before sending is a basic precaution and takes seconds.

Relevance comes next. Sending a two-hundred-page manual when the recipient needs one chapter wastes their time and buries the part that matters.

Cleanliness is the third. Fax cover sheets, scanner test pages, blank duplex backs, and duplicate scans all accumulate in documents that pass through several hands. Removing them makes the file look deliberate rather than assembled by accident.

How Page Numbering Works Here

This is worth being explicit about, because it is the single most common source of mistakes. Every number you enter refers to the original document exactly as it is now. If you ask to remove pages 3, 7, and 12, the tool looks at the document as a whole, marks those three pages, and removes them together. It does not delete page 3 first and then renumber everything so that what was page 4 becomes page 3.

The practical consequence is that you can read the page numbers straight off your PDF reader and type them in without doing any arithmetic. Some tools require you to compensate for shifting numbers; this one does not.

Delete, Extract, or Split — Which One?

Delete keeps everything except what you name, returning a single file. Best when the document is nearly right and only a few pages need to go.

Extract Pages keeps only what you name and discards the rest, also returning a single file. Best when you want a small section from a long document.

Split PDF divides the document into several output files. Best when you need each part to exist separately.

Delete and extract are mirror images of each other, so pick whichever requires typing fewer numbers. Removing three pages from a two-hundred-page report is a deletion; keeping three pages out of two hundred is an extraction.

How to Delete Pages from a PDF

Upload your PDF by dragging it onto the box or clicking to browse. Type the pages you want removed, using commas for individual pages and hyphens for ranges — 1, 4, 8-11 is a valid entry. Check the numbers against your document, then click to process and your trimmed PDF downloads immediately.

It is worth opening the result once to confirm the right pages went. The original file on your computer is never modified, so if the selection was wrong you can simply upload it again and try different numbers.

Smaller Files, Faster Sharing

Every page removed takes its content with it, which in a scanned document means a full-page image of several hundred kilobytes each time. Deleting twenty blank or unwanted pages from a scan can cut the file size dramatically — often enough on its own to get under an email attachment limit.
