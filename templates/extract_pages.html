Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose which pages to extract?
Enter the page numbers separated by commas, using a hyphen for a continuous run. 1, 3, 10-15 extracts page one, page three, and pages ten through fifteen. Everything you do not list is left out of the new file.
Do the extracted pages keep their original order?
Yes. Pages appear in the new document in ascending order regardless of the sequence you typed them in, so entering 10, 2, 5 produces a file with pages 2, 5, and 10 in that order. To control the sequence yourself, use Organize PDF after extracting.
What is the difference between extracting and splitting?
Extracting produces one new PDF containing only the pages you chose. Split PDF divides the document into several separate files. Extract when you want a single focused document; split when you need each section to exist as its own file.
Is the original document changed in any way?
No. Your uploaded file is never modified. Extraction reads from it and builds a completely new document, so the original on your computer stays exactly as it was and you can extract a different selection from it as many times as you like.
Will the extracted pages lose quality?
No. Each page is copied across exactly as it exists in the source. Embedded fonts stay embedded, text remains selectable and searchable, vector graphics stay sharp at any zoom, and images keep their original resolution with no recompression.
Can I extract from a password-protected PDF?
Yes. A password prompt appears automatically after upload if the document is encrypted. Enter it once and extraction proceeds normally. Our Unlock PDF tool can also remove the password permanently beforehand.

Extract Pages from PDF Online — Free PDF Page Extractor

You rarely need an entire document. You need the invoice on page fourteen, the chapter that runs from forty to sixty-two, or the three signed pages buried in a hundred-page contract. Extraction pulls exactly those pages into a new PDF and leaves everything else behind. It is free, instant, and does not touch your original file.

Why Extract Instead of Sending the Whole File?

Focus. A recipient who opens a four-page extract reads it. The same person handed a four-hundred-page manual and told the relevant part is "somewhere in section nine" often does not. Sending only what matters is a courtesy that noticeably improves response rates.

Size. Extracting a handful of pages from a large scanned document produces a file a fraction of the original size — frequently the simplest way to get an attachment under an email limit.

Confidentiality. Sometimes the safest approach is not to remove the sensitive pages but to extract only the ones you are willing to share. Nothing else can be recovered from a file that never contained it.

Extract, Delete, or Split — Which Do You Need?

Extract keeps only the pages you name and returns one new file. Best when you want a small piece of a large document.

Delete Pages keeps everything except the pages you name, also returning one file. Best when the document is nearly right and a few pages need removing.

Split PDF divides the document into several output files at once. Best when every section needs to exist independently.

Extract and delete are mirror images, so choose the one that means typing fewer numbers. Wanting five pages out of three hundred is clearly an extraction; wanting to lose five out of three hundred is a deletion.

How to Extract Pages from a PDF

Upload your PDF by dragging it onto the box or clicking to browse. Enter the pages you want, using commas for individual pages and hyphens for ranges — 2, 7, 20-25 is a valid entry. Confirm the numbers against your document, then process and your new PDF downloads immediately.

Because the original is never modified, extraction is entirely non-destructive. If you got the range wrong, upload the same file again and enter different numbers. Nothing is at risk.

Page Order in the Result

Extracted pages appear in ascending numerical order regardless of the sequence you typed them in. Entering 15, 3, 8 produces a document with pages 3, 8, and 15 in that order rather than the order you listed. This is almost always what people want, since the pages then read in their original sequence. If you specifically need a different arrangement, run the extracted file through Organize PDF, where you can drag pages into any order you like.

Quality Is Preserved Exactly

Pages are copied rather than rebuilt. Embedded fonts stay embedded, so text renders identically and remains fully selectable, searchable, and copyable. Vector graphics stay sharp at any zoom level. Photographs and scans keep the exact resolution they had in the source document. The extracted pages are indistinguishable from the originals.
