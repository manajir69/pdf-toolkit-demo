Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and files

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PDF Toolkit Demo ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our online PDF tools at example.com.

2. Files You Upload

Your privacy is our top priority when it comes to your files:

  • We do not store your uploaded PDF or image files on our servers permanently.
  • Files are processed in memory and deleted immediately after your download is complete.
  • We do not access, read, share, or sell the content of your files.
  • We strongly recommend that you do not upload files containing highly sensitive personal data.

3. Information We Collect

We may collect limited non-personal technical data to improve our service, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website

This data is collected anonymously and is used only to understand how our tools are being used so we can improve them.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the functionality of our tools.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as hosting providers and analytics tools. These services may collect anonymized data according to their own privacy policies. We do not share your uploaded files with any third party.

6. Data Security

We take appropriate technical measures to protect your data during transmission. All connections to PDF Toolkit Demo use HTTPS encryption. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: Contact Page
