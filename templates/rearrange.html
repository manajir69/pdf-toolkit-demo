Frequently Asked Questions

How do I move a page to a new position?
Drag its thumbnail and drop it where you want it. The surrounding pages shift to make room and the numbering updates immediately, so the layout on screen always shows exactly what the finished document will look like.
Can I reorder pages on a phone or tablet?
Dragging works on touchscreens, though it can be fiddly with many pages. For precise control on a small screen, Organize PDF gives every page left and right buttons that move it one position at a time without any dragging.
Does reordering affect quality or file size?
No. Pages are copied into their new positions exactly as they exist in the source document. Embedded fonts, image resolution, and vector graphics are all untouched, and the file size stays essentially the same since the same content is simply written in a different sequence.
Can I reorder and rotate pages at the same time?
Not on this page — this tool focuses on order alone. Organize PDF combines reordering with rotation and deletion in a single visual editor, so all your changes happen in one upload and one download instead of three separate rounds.
What happens to bookmarks and internal links?
Internal page links are remapped to follow the pages they point at. Bookmarks in complex documents may need checking after a substantial reorganisation, so it is worth opening the result and testing the navigation if your document relies heavily on a table of contents.
Can I reorder a password-protected PDF?
Yes. A password prompt appears automatically after upload if the file is encrypted. Enter it once and the thumbnails render as normal. To remove the password permanently instead, use our Unlock PDF tool first.

Rearrange PDF Pages Online — Free Drag and Drop Page Reorder

Pages end up in the wrong order constantly — a scanner picked up the stack upside down, a merge joined files in the wrong sequence, or a document was simply assembled out of order. This tool shows every page as a thumbnail you can drag into position, so you fix the sequence visually rather than by typing page numbers and hoping. It is free and runs entirely in your browser.

Why Page Order Goes Wrong

Scanning is the usual culprit. Sheet feeders take paper from the top of the stack, so a bundle loaded face-up produces a document running backwards from the last page to the first. Reloading the feeder halfway through a long job interleaves the batches incorrectly.

Merging is the other common cause. Files joined in alphabetical order rarely land in the order the content should actually read, particularly when names begin with dates or numbers that sort in unexpected ways.

Late additions account for the rest. An appendix gets appended at the end when it belongs after chapter three; a signature page arrives separately and gets tacked on wherever there was room.

Why Dragging Beats Typing

Reordering by typing a sequence like 1,5,2,3,4 works, but only if you already know precisely which page is which. With scanned documents you usually do not — page seventeen might be the start of a chapter or the middle of a table, and there is no way to tell from the number alone.

Seeing thumbnails removes the guesswork entirely. You recognise a page by looking at it, drag it where it belongs, and watch the numbering update as you go. Mistakes become obvious immediately instead of after you have downloaded the file and opened it.

Who Uses This Tool

Anyone working with scans puts reverse-order documents back the right way round. Students assemble lecture notes, readings, and handouts into a coherent study document. Office teams reorder merged reports so sections follow the intended structure. Legal and administrative staff arrange bundle pages to match a filed index exactly. Anyone preparing something for print checks the sequence visually before committing paper to it.

How to Rearrange PDF Pages

Upload your PDF by dragging it onto the box or clicking to browse. Every page renders as a thumbnail within a few seconds. Drag any page and drop it where it belongs — the pages around it shift to make room and the numbers update live. When the order on screen matches what you want, download the reordered file.

Nothing is applied to your document until you download, so you can experiment freely and start over at any point.

Reordering Is Completely Lossless

Changing page order does not change page content. Each page is copied into its new position exactly as it exists in the source — embedded fonts stay embedded, text remains selectable and searchable, vector graphics stay sharp at any zoom, and images keep their original resolution. File size is essentially unchanged, since the same content is simply written in a different sequence.

Reorder Alone, or Everything Together?

If order is the only problem, this tool is the quickest route. But documents that arrived in the wrong order often have other issues from the same source — pages that fed in sideways, blank backs from duplex scanning, a duplicate sheet or two. In that case Organize PDF saves several rounds of work by combining reordering, rotation, and deletion in a single editor with one upload and one download.

Your Privacy Is Protected

Thumbnails are rendered locally in your browser, so the visual editing happens entirely on your own machine. When you download, the file is processed in memory and discarded immediately afterwards. Nothing is written to disk, nothing is logged, and no copy is retained.
